# 🏆 Fifa Fantasy APSJ Rules & Scoring

Welcome to prediction dashboard APSJ Boys!

Read the guidelines below carefully to maximize your points. Remember, all daily submissions lock **15 minutes before the first match of the day kicks off (IST).**

## 🚀 Part 1: Pre-Tournament Lock-Ins (League Stage)

Before the tournament begins, you must lock in your core selections. These choices are your heavy hitters for the entire league stage.

### 1. Pre-T Players (Pick 5) 

Your chosen 5 foundational players earn **Double Points** for baseline attacking stats during the league stage.

- **Goal Scored:** +20 Points (Instead of the usual 10)
- **Man of the Match (MotM):** +40 Points (Instead of the usual 20)

### 2. Pre-T Teams (Pick 2)

Your 2 baseline favorite teams earn **Double Points** for every victory during the league stage.

- **Match Win:** +20 Points (Instead of the usual 10)

## 📅 Part 2: Daily Matchday Predictions

Every single matchday, you must log in and submit your picks before the daily countdown cuts off.

### 1. Match Winners

Predict the winner or **DRAW** for each specific match scheduled for the day using the dropdown menus.

- **The Default Rule:** If one of your 2 Pre-T Teams is playing today, the system automatically locks them in as your predicted winner for that match.
- **Daily Stats Value:**
  - **Correct Prediction:** +10 Points per match

💡 **Example:** : You predict a standard team to win. If they win the match, you earn 10 points.  
If your Pre-T Team is playing today, so the system automatically locks them in as your predicted winner. If they win the match, the Pre-T Double Points rule overrides the standard value, earning you 20 points instead of 10!  
_Note: There will be no option to select Draw when pre-T team is playing._

### 2. Daily Performance Players (Pick 2)

Every day, you must select 2 players you think will shine.

- **The Default Rule:** If one of your 5 Pre-T Players is playing today, the system automatically locks them in as your daily selection.
- **Daily Stats Value:**
  - **Goal Scored:** +10 Points
  - **Man of the Match (MotM):** +20 Points  

💡 **Example:** If you choose Mbappe as a daily pick and he scores 1 goal and wins MotM, you get 30 points ($10 + 20$).  
If Mbappe happens to be your Pre-T Player, the rule from Part 1 overrides this, and you get 60 points ($20 + 40$)!

## 📈 Part 3: Advanced Scoring & Penalties

### 1. Knock-Out Stage Multipliers

When the tournament moves to the high-stakes knockout rounds, loyalty pays off:

- **The Loyalty Bonus:** If you stick with your original Pre-T Teams and Players and they advance, their points for wins and goals are Doubled again!
- **The Transfer Strategy:** If you decide to swap your teams out for stronger remaining contenders in the knockouts, your points for those new selections will use a 1.5x Multiplier instead of the full double bonus.

### 2. Goal Difference Bonus & Penalty (Under Review)

Your score shifts dynamically based on how dominant your predicted team is on the pitch.

- **If your predicted team WINS:** You get +10 Points per goal difference.
- **If your predicted team LOSES:** You get -10 Points per goal difference.
- _Note: This goal difference impact is completely Doubled if the team playing is one of your Pre-T Teams._  

💡 Example: You predict your Pre-T Team to win, but they lose the match $1 - 4$ (a 3-goal difference). You incur a penalty of -60 points ($3 \text{ goals} \times -10 \text{ points} \times 2 \text{ for Pre-T status}$).

### 3. Discipline Penalties:

Keep an eye on player behavior. Rough tackles will cost you on the leaderboard.

- **Yellow Card:** -5 Points (Doubled to -10 Points for Pre-T Players)
- **Red Card:** -10 Points (Doubled to -20 Points for Pre-T Players)
